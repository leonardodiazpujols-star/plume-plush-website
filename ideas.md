## Chosen Approach: Dark Opulence × Pink Marble Editorial

**Theme Name:** Dark Opulence × Pink Marble Editorial

**Very Brief Intro:** A cinematic luxury editorial experience that pairs deep charcoal marble drama with warm blush-pink and champagne gold accents — evoking the precision of a master artisan's atelier.

**Design Movement:** Neo-Baroque Luxury Editorial — the visual language of Vogue Paris meets a Parisian atelier. Dark, dramatic, yet feminine and warm.

**Core Principles:**
1. Charcoal marble hero sections anchor the page with drama; pink marble sections provide warmth and contrast
2. Gold is the only accent color — used for typography highlights, dividers, icons, and CTAs
3. Every section transition uses a subtle marble texture shift to maintain visual continuity
4. Live artisan photography (hands, tools, process) creates authenticity and tactile richness

**Color Philosophy:**
- Charcoal Black: `#1a1a1a` / `oklch(0.14 0.005 285)` — primary dark background
- Blush Pink Marble: `#d4a0a0` / `oklch(0.72 0.06 15)` — warm accent sections
- Champagne Gold: `#c9a84c` / `oklch(0.72 0.09 75)` — all gold elements
- Cream Ivory: `#f5f0e8` / `oklch(0.96 0.01 80)` — light section backgrounds
- Deep Rose: `#8b4a5a` / `oklch(0.45 0.08 15)` — deep pink for text on light

**Layout Paradigm:** Asymmetric editorial grid — alternating full-bleed dark and light sections, with off-center content blocks, editorial-style image crops, and generous vertical rhythm.

**Signature Elements:**
1. Gold feather divider line (— 🪶 —) between sections
2. Charcoal marble texture as hero background with pink vein highlights
3. Overlapping P&P monogram as watermark/favicon

**Interaction Philosophy:** Smooth, unhurried scroll-triggered reveals. Hover states use gold underlines and subtle scale transforms. CTAs have a gold border that fills on hover.

**Animation:** Fade-up entrance animations at 400ms ease-out. Parallax on hero background. Staggered card reveals. Nav becomes opaque charcoal on scroll.

**Typography System:**
- Display: Cormorant Garamond (ultra-light/italic for headlines) — the voice of heritage luxury
- Body: Jost (300/400 weight) — clean, modern, readable
- Accent: Cormorant Garamond SC (small caps) — for labels, taglines, eyebrows

**Brand Essence:** Where artistry meets prestige — for luxury brands who demand the exceptional. Refined, global, irreplaceable.

**Brand Voice:** Headlines are poetic and declarative. CTAs are invitations, not commands. Microcopy is understated and precise.
- Example headline: "Where Artistry Meets Prestige"
- Example CTA: "Discover the World of P&P"

**Wordmark & Logo:** Overlapping P&P in charcoal marble with liquid gold ampersand. "PLUME & PLUSH" in Cormorant Garamond wide-tracked caps below.

**Signature Brand Color:** Champagne Gold `#c9a84c`
